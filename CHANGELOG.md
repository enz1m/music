# Changelog

## 0.2.1

- Switch between your library and playback views with smoother sidebar transitions and easier-to-find view controls.
- Use your desktop’s media controls with Music’s own player entry.

## 0.2.0

- Play local music, podcasts, and internet radio, and control Spotify Connect.
- Organize playlists and the playback queue, view listening history, and tune
  playback with an equalizer and ReplayGain.
- Add Flatpak packaging and update to enzUI Alpha 0.3.
